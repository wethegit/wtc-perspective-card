wtc-perspective-card

A vanilla JS perspective card with hover, ambient, and clickable modes.

GitHub · Docs

Hover

Tilts toward the cursor on hover.

Ambient

data-ambient: animates continuously while in view.

Clickable

Flips and zooms into a modal. Click or Enter opens; Esc closes.

Holographic foil

A custom foil over image artwork, swung by the tilt custom properties the card publishes.

Custom button

Brings its own button, and rests face-down (data-start-flipped) until opened.

Custom dialog

Opens into its own <dialog> (data-dialog) and shares the modal with a content panel.

Featured card

Abyssal Wanderer

A custom dialog lets the card share its modal with arbitrary content — copy, metadata, calls to action. The card still flies in and flips as usual; everything around it is your own markup and CSS.
